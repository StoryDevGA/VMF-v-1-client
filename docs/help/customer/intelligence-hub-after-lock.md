# Intelligence Hub After lock

A locked revision is a frozen reference. New material can be recorded separately only through an authorised post-lock submission path. Recording it does not change the locked evidence basis, accept the material or create another revision.

## What the current workspace offers

After lock provides lock-basis inspection and a list of saved post-lock submissions when the server can verify them. Open a saved item to inspect its original receipt, source details, impact decisions and any recorded successor admission. Review and admission controls appear only when the current server checks and your authority permit them. New submissions are prepared separately in Context. An unavailable inbox is not a zero-item result.

The recording-availability explanation reports a separate server check for the selected revision and agrees between the screen and its inspection dialog. Loading, known blockers, denied access and an unavailable check are different results. While a check is pending or cannot be verified, an earlier result does not establish current availability. Satisfied prerequisites do not enable a submission control on this screen.

The same recording-availability explanation is also shown for a locked revision in [Context](intelligence-hub-context.md) and Workspace Structure. It uses each screen's current selected revision and server read. Recording availability is separate from authority to inspect, review or admit an existing item. If a required check is denied or unavailable, the affected action stays blocked. No screen unlocks acquisition or changes frozen evidence.

**Gather separate evidence** opens [Context](intelligence-hub-context.md) in the same selected revision. Acquisition remains disabled while that revision is locked. Where permitted, import a supplied source-and-evidence JSON candidate, inspect it, enter a reason and explicitly submit through the separate form. Check its immediate saved receipt in Context, then use After lock to reopen saved submissions and inspect their later history. Use only actions that the current workspace and server explicitly permit.

## Check the exact locked basis

The lock-basis inspector checks recorded lock metadata and any matching replay anchor for shape and agreement. It does not independently verify the original snapshot body or replay integrity. The separate recording-availability check does not turn this inspector into a new verification of those original bodies. Saved submissions and their exact-item review/admission history are inspected separately on After lock.

Check the selected workspace, revision and recorded lock before interpreting new material. The recorded snapshot identifies the protected basis; upload time, current source inventory and graph records cannot replace exact frozen membership and evidence versions.

Missing membership and failed verification are different conditions. A revision with no recorded frozen membership cannot claim that basis. Conflicting or unverifiable recorded material requires investigation, rather than treating it as an empty history. Loading and failed refreshes do not establish a current result.

An availability check describes what the server verified at that time. It does not grant permission or promise that a later request will succeed. The server checks the current rules again when an explicit submission is made. A missing policy, insufficient update permission or an incomplete required check keeps new recording blocked.

## Understand a submitted receipt

When a governed post-lock journey is available, a successful submission records the supplied evidence and its source outside the locked snapshot. Keep its receipt and request reference. The record retains source identity, location, content hash, retrieval/currentness information, qualifications, licensing and provenance. A record of origin is not acceptance, approval or proof that every required check has passed.

A list of submitted receipts describes original recording events. **Pending review** on an original intake receipt is not a current pending-item count: the same receipt can remain after a later review or admission. A receipt list alone does not establish the latest review or admission outcome. Inspect the exact item's verified history before deciding what happens next.

Authorised inspection of a retained receipt can remain possible when new intake is disabled. An old receipt does not restore update permission or enable another submission. Missing or unverifiable review/admission proof must not be interpreted as a verified empty history or proof that no successor was ever recorded. A historical successor link records what happened; it does not certify that successor's present evidence basis or readiness.

## Review impact separately

Impact review concerns the exact locked basis and submitted item. An authorised reviewer with the existing workspace update permission must record a rationale. The four governed conclusions are:

- **No revision impact**: record that the material does not require a successor on this review basis.
- **Corroborative**: record supporting material without changing the frozen basis.
- **Successor revision required**: identify the need for separate successor work.
- **Currentness review required**: require further currentness review; this does not permit admission.

No result is selected automatically. A missing decision awaits review; an unverified decision is not a safe basis for admission. Ordinary evidence acceptance is separate from impact review and cannot silently add evidence to frozen truth.

Open the saved item, inspect its current impact history, choose a conclusion, enter the reason and explicitly confirm before selecting **Record impact decision**. Use **Refresh impact history** to read the current history again. A saved decision includes its reviewer, reason and audit reference; an uncertain response must be checked before another action.

## Admit only through an explicit successor action

Only a latest verified **Successor revision required** decision can support a separate admission request. The authorised person must explicitly confirm it and provide an admission rationale. The normal revision workflow creates the successor's own evidence basis while preserving the locked predecessor.

The admitted material remains a candidate for ordinary evidence review and acceptance. Its retained origin connects the original submission, source qualifications/provenance, selected impact decision and admission record. Admission does not approve evidence, certify readiness or publish or lock the successor. A currentness concern, expired retention permission or stale decision/basis keeps admission blocked.

## Inspect the successor in Workspace Structure

Use the recorded successor link to open that revision. **Revision relationship and status** shows the selected revision's own readiness, publication state and state version, with verified predecessor or successor links where available. **Refresh revision status** reads that status again; it does not create a revision or rerun the VMF. A draft successor can be not ready and not published even when its predecessor is locked and published. Generation and acceptance remain disabled on the locked predecessor. Further work in the successor follows its normal permissions and readiness checks.

## Recover without creating another uncertain operation

If the selected basis changes, refresh the exact workspace and item, then inspect its current reason before deliberately trying again. A stale request is rejected; do not work around it by using another view or revision.

If a submission outcome is unconfirmed, use **Check saved submission** in Context while its original request is retained. This reads the original result; it does not send another submission. For an uncertain decision or admission, use the original-result check offered by that panel and inspect the recorded history. Do not automatically retry or start a fresh request while the original outcome remains uncertain. Reusing a request key with changed material is a conflict, not an update. A failed or unavailable read does not prove that nothing was saved. If the original request is no longer available, retain its references and ask for support before another operation.

Use [Sources](intelligence-hub-sources.md) for available provenance inspection and [Readiness & publish](intelligence-hub-readiness-publish.md) for the separate publication conditions. Public Research acquisition and provider availability remain separate from this generic post-lock process.
