# Intelligence Hub Readiness & publish

This view presents readiness and publication conditions for the selected revision. Publication and locking are performed in Workspace Structure.

## Separate the checks

Discovery readiness, Outcome Studio readiness, evidence review, Truth Quality certification, human control decisions and Draft / Published / Locked lifecycle are distinct facts. A machine check or certification cannot create human approval.

Recorded Discovery readiness comes from the existing scoped Discovery Health assessment shared with Coverage and Quality. Inspect its recorded blockers, qualifications and assessment/read times. Required evidence refresh marks the assessment stale; missing refresh information stays unknown. No refresh marker does not prove input currency, and the assessment input basis is unavailable from this read. Recorded Ready does not establish every output's sufficiency, Review Completion or human authority. The assurance report includes this qualified assessment and refreshes its read; loading or failed reads withhold prior values.

A current Review Completion receipt is a publication condition. Named human controls need the recorded decision, rationale, authorised actor, time and exact revision / evidence / snapshot basis. Unavailable controls are not approvals. A complete snapshot does not establish readiness for every output.

The human control register contains Review completion, Exception disclosure and Publication authorisation. Review completion shows only a recorded current or stale human receipt, or explicitly no receipt/unavailable. Accepted evidence counts and a complete decision population alone do not establish this control.

Exception disclosure requires accurate disclosure of known gaps, contradictions, limitations and permitted exceptions. A no-known-exceptions decision requires a complete current basis; disclosure cannot waive mandatory requirements. Publication authorisation requires a current human grant for the exact package action and its actual server authority in Workspace Structure. Grant/refusal/withdrawal receipts for those two controls are currently unavailable, so neither is presented as approved. Their history is separate from general runtime activity and Outcome Studio asset approvals.

The Review Completion panel shows the current mandatory-decision population and the recorded receipt. Inspect its rationale, reviewer and audit reference here; return to Review to record a completion. A current receipt can coexist with missing accepted section truth or unavailable output readiness. Relevant evidence or decision changes can make a receipt stale and require reassessment.

## Inspect the recorded basis

Recorded lock snapshot and the assurance report inspect the same scoped persisted lock basis: snapshot ID/hash/time, actor/version and a matching recorded replay anchor where available. A recorded hash is not independent verification of the original snapshot body or replay integrity. Frozen source membership and exact evidence versions remain unavailable if the lock producer did not record them. Loading, wrong-scope, mismatched-version and failed reads hide prior metadata; Refresh retries the selected scope without executing an action.

Current evidence inventory shows expected/read evidence and source counts from the scoped complete receipt. Inspect inventory receipt shows its hash, exact revision/state version, read time and source records. Stored-reference mapping can remain unresolved while the inventory is complete; that does not establish missing evidence or publication readiness. This is a current stored inventory, separate from the frozen Lock snapshot and selected-target readiness. Refresh hides old values and rereads the exact scope.

Use source scope, evidence decisions, Quality findings and snapshot history to inspect the displayed basis. Preview assurance report opens a read-only inspection of the exact workspace, revision, customer and tenant. It shows validated current inventory counts/hash/read time, canonical source and processing facts, current graph coverage/hash and recorded lifecycle/snapshot identity where supplied. Opening time is separate from receipt read times; the report updates with current reads and withholds facts while those reads refresh or fail. Its separate read receipts do not create an atomic approval or frozen report.

Missing certification, named human approvals, complete history, frozen evidence membership/version proof and selected-target readiness remain disclosed as unavailable. Inspect the Review Completion receipt separately in this view. Recorded graph coverage or a lock snapshot identity cannot establish human approval. Export controls remain disabled when a governed export capability is unavailable. General activity is not dedicated human approval history.

The report also inspects the existing Review Completion population and recorded receipt/history. It shows the human rationale, reviewer, recorded authority, time, population hash/policy, observed state version and audit anchors. Current or stale receipt status follows the current decision population; an older observed state version alone does not make a receipt stale. Confirmed contradictions can have completed decisions and still block readiness. A complete decision population without a recorded receipt is not human approval.

Review Completion history returns at most 25 receipts and explicitly identifies partial history when more records exist. These receipts do not substitute for complete named control history, exception disclosure or publication authorisation. The report displays audit identifiers/signature versions as recorded anchors; it does not verify audit signatures. **Refresh report** rereads its current scoped subscriptions, including the graph and review basis, without accepting evidence, recording a decision, recomputing, publishing or locking.

Current revision source basis shows the canonical unique-source total and qualified Documents processed count shared across the Hub. Successful current-content document receipts count once per source; unknown proof makes the whole processed total unavailable and known stale documents are excluded. This is separate from the Lock snapshot card. A recorded snapshot identity does not provide its frozen source inventory: that inventory stays unavailable until its verified membership receipt is supplied. Current source or evidence totals cannot substitute for frozen membership or publication readiness.

Where a readiness API or current receipt is unavailable, the screen says so. Refresh rereads bounded facts; it does not certify, recompute, accept, publish or lock.

## Publication handoff

The Workspace Structure link opens the exact selected workspace revision. Actual action availability and authority come from that governed workspace's runtime action projection. A navigation link is not permission to publish or lock, and Hub has no Publish & Lock execution control.

Return to [Review](intelligence-hub-review.md) for outstanding decisions or inspect [After lock](intelligence-hub-after-lock.md) for the frozen-snapshot boundary.
