# Intelligence Hub Evidence readiness

Evidence readiness sits after Review and explains the handoff from evidence to understanding. It keeps acceptance, snapshot inventory, interpretation, target sufficiency and lifecycle separate.

## What the state means

Current inventory and Records read use the complete scoped inventory receipt. Advanced evidence inspection shows its expected/read evidence and source counts, current inventory hash, exact revision and state version, read time and source records. Unresolved stored section references leave mapping unassessed; they do not change a complete inventory into missing evidence. This receipt covers current stored records, including on a locked revision. It does not establish frozen lock membership, a previous successful interpretation, selected-target sufficiency or approval. Refresh retries an unavailable receipt and hides previous values while the current scope is being checked.

A complete inventory must bind to a scoped snapshot identity, hash, expected/read counts, source inventory and currency. Completeness alone does not prove that evidence supports a required framework section, output or plan. Required and optional support depend on the explicit selected target.

Unavailable or mapping-blocked means sufficiency could not be assessed; it is not automatically missing customer evidence. Accepted does not mean Verified. Six classification counts are unavailable until their governed definitions and counting rules are connected.

Verified requires a current authorised validation record for a named assertion, check and evidence basis. Reported attributes a statement to its source without implying independent verification. Hypothesis explicitly labels an unvalidated assumption or approved interpretation and preserves its origin. Unresolved requires a recorded material question or dependency; unknown classification is separate. Contradicted requires a current confirmed contradiction, rather than a detector candidate. Requires confirmation identifies a specific outstanding requirement and its required actor/action.

Each bucket counts unique current evidence IDs within the declared revision/snapshot scope, with assertion and version qualifications. Reported can overlap verification or confirmation; Hypothesis, Unresolved and Requires confirmation may also overlap. Buckets need not sum to the total, and a validated assertion does not make a whole multi-assertion record Verified. Verified and Contradicted on the same assertion and current basis require reconciliation. These labels do not grant readiness; the selected target's policy determines blocking conditions. The current view shows unavailable counts rather than deriving labels from evidence acceptance or titles.

Sources connected and Documents processed use the current revision's canonical source summary, shared with Overview, Context and Sources. Source totals remain independent from evidence-page completeness. Documents processed counts distinct current uploaded-document sources with successful receipts matching their current content, including successful zero-object extraction. Unknown proof makes the whole processed total unavailable; known stale documents are excluded. Loading or failed reads hide previous values until the exact scope is refreshed. These current records do not establish frozen snapshot membership, interpretation or target sufficiency.

## Use truthful handoffs

Inspect sources opens Sources and performs no synchronisation. Add evidence and strengthen-evidence links open Context. Findings are resolved in Quality. The current recomputation panel reports unavailable when no governed progress or lineage receipt is supplied; navigation does not recompute or approve anything.

Recently added evidence and decision-anchor assessments require exact versions and recorded basis. Where those projections are unavailable, a generic inspection destination does not prove that the exact changed item or source gap has been identified.

Inspect source records opens the general scoped Sources registry; it does not identify recently changed evidence. Inspect coverage opens the domain inspection without asserting an assessed source gap. The Context links open acquisition inspection without imposing decision or finance requirements. Exact decision-anchor controls remain disabled while no recorded anchor or item-specific handoff is supplied.

## Continue in the selected scope

Recorded lock snapshot describes persisted metadata, rather than a verified successful evidence snapshot. Open Advanced evidence inspection to see its exact ID, hash, time, actor, version and matching recorded replay anchor where available. Original-body integrity, frozen membership and exact evidence versions are separate proof and remain unavailable when no receipt was recorded. Loading or failed reads hide old metadata; Refresh retries the current scope.

The back footer opens [Review](intelligence-hub-review.md). The forward footer opens [Readiness & publish](intelligence-hub-readiness-publish.md). Both retain the selected revision, including locked and unavailable states. Expanded diagnostics are inspection only.

Resolve the displayed current reason before continuing. A Review Completion receipt, source snapshot receipt or target-readiness assessment cannot be inferred from a green count.
