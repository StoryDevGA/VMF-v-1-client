# Intelligence Hub Coverage

Coverage explains the existing ten-domain DIG map and its governed states. The metric labelled Domain/Intelligence Coverage is the share of the ten DIG domains with connected accepted evidence. It is separate from evidence-object counts, evidence acceptance, confidence and publication readiness.

## Read the basis

Overview and Coverage use the same current, revision-scoped graph manifest. Its recorded coverage describes how many of the ten DIG domains have connected accepted evidence. A missing, stale, failed or inconsistent manifest leaves coverage unavailable; Refresh rereads the selected revision. No legacy graph or acquisition score substitutes for that receipt. This current graph basis is separate from frozen snapshot membership, target sufficiency and publication approval.

A domain can have supporting evidence from multiple unique sources. Where the current graph records the optional connected-source facts, Coverage counts distinct recorded source identities for connected accepted evidence and displays their recorded source kinds. One source is counted once within a domain and may support several domains. Recorded kinds do not certify independent corroboration, source authority or copied-origin independence. Unknown kinds are reported separately.

Unresolved source provenance leaves the total unavailable and shows the resolved identities as a lower bound. A distribution exceeding twelve recorded kinds is unavailable rather than silently truncated. Older snapshots without these facts retain unavailable source counts and kinds; inspection does not rebuild a locked snapshot. Arbitrary per-domain percentage scores do not explain confidence. Evidence volume alone does not establish support.

Discovery readiness comes from Discovery Health. Missing domain rationale, source-diversity or confidence projections remain unavailable until supplied by the governed contracts. A visible bounded subset is not the whole revision's evidence population.

The Discovery readiness card uses the same scoped recorded assessment as Quality and Readiness & publish. It shows recorded blockers, qualifications, assessment time and read time. A recorded refresh requirement marks it stale. Missing refresh status remains unknown; no refresh marker does not verify its input currency. The assessment input basis is unavailable from this read. Recorded Ready does not establish Review Completion, certification, human approval or readiness for every output. Refresh withholds old values while the read is loading or unavailable.

## Improve evidence

Acquisition handoffs open [Context](intelligence-hub-context.md), preserving the selected revision. Supporting-evidence inspection belongs to [Sources](intelligence-hub-sources.md); exact domain / evidence filters must be retained where available.

The selected domain and coverage filter are recorded in the scoped link for reload, Back and Help return. A requested domain outside the current filter or current read is explained without substituting another domain. Choose a displayed domain or change the filter to recover. Invalid or mismatched inspection context has its own reset notice.

**Search sources and evidence for this domain** performs a complete revision-scoped free-text search using the domain name. It does not certify structured domain membership or choose an exact finding. Previous exact-source filters and unrelated Graph return markers are cleared. **Open acquisition in Context** and **Open Intelligence Quality** are navigation; absent domain-specific recommendations remain unavailable.

A recommendation is guidance. It does not acquire a source, accept evidence, resolve a finding or certify readiness. Locked inspection stays available; changes use the permitted normal revision workflow.

Use [Intelligence Graph](intelligence-hub-graph.md) to inspect delivered relationships and [Intelligence Quality](intelligence-quality.md) for governed finding decisions.
