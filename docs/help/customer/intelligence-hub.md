# Intelligence Hub

Intelligence Hub brings the selected workspace revision's evidence, review and readiness into one place. Check the workspace name and revision before following an action. A locked revision remains a recorded reference.

## Follow the evidence journey

- [Context](intelligence-hub-context.md) owns the acquisition brief, websites and documents.
- [Sources](intelligence-hub-sources.md) connects each evidence object to its canonical source.
- [Review](intelligence-hub-review.md) brings outstanding decisions together. [Intelligence Quality](intelligence-quality.md) resolves supported findings.
- [Evidence readiness](intelligence-hub-evidence-readiness.md) explains the evidence-to-understanding handoff.
- [Readiness & publish](intelligence-hub-readiness-publish.md) presents publication conditions. Execution belongs to Workspace Structure.
- [After lock](intelligence-hub-after-lock.md) inspects changes against a frozen revision. [Coverage](intelligence-hub-coverage.md) and [Intelligence Graph](intelligence-hub-graph.md) explain the evidence basis.

**Public research availability.**

Context shows an unchecked, disabled **Research public information** control. **Research availability** opens an explanation; it does not open configuration, save research settings or start a research run. Live public research and controlled replay remain unavailable. Continue using the existing website and document intake where authorised. Staging inputs, saving a brief or refreshing a view does not start public research. Acquisition creates candidates for Sources and Review; it does not accept evidence, recompute understanding or change a locked revision.

## Read summaries carefully

Overview contains Intelligence Assurance, Sources, Evidence Objects, Evidence Coverage and Items Recommended for Review above its three detailed cards. Tabs keep the same position when you switch views. Counts describe their stated population; acceptance, confidence, quality disposition, completeness and approval are separate facts.

Items recommended for review currently counts outstanding contradiction decisions from the complete revision review population. Current Not Contradictory and Confirmed dispositions are decided and excluded; a confirmed contradiction can still independently block readiness. Other finding types are unavailable in this metric. Missing, stale or failed reads show Unavailable rather than a saved acquisition candidate count. Open Intelligence Quality to inspect and resolve findings; Refresh rereads the count.

Evidence Coverage uses the current scoped graph manifest's recorded ten-domain coverage. Select its value to inspect the covered-domain basis; Check evidence coverage opens Coverage. Missing or stale graph coverage remains unavailable and does not fall back to a saved acquisition score. Domain coverage is separate from Evidence readiness, target sufficiency and publication approval.

Overview, Context and Sources share the same whole-revision source summary. Sources counts unique source identities, rather than evidence objects or visible rows. Documents processed counts distinct current uploaded-document sources with a verified successful acquisition receipt for their current content. A successful extraction with zero eligible evidence objects counts once; it does not prove evidence sufficiency. Known stale documents are excluded from the current processed count. Missing or unverified proof makes the whole processed total unavailable; verified, unknown and stale counts explain that incomplete basis.

Unavailable means the required fact or contract could not be read. Partial describes a bounded subset. Stale describes an older basis. None establishes a complete or ready revision. Use the displayed reason and recovery rather than assuming a missing value is zero.

## Recover safely

Add Evidence opens Context. A locked revision permits inspection but blocks acquisition and truth changes. Use the authorised normal revision workflow when new acquisition is needed. Refresh rereads the selected scope; it does not approve evidence, publish, lock or create a successor revision.

Some governed receipts and projections are not yet connected. Disabled decisions and unavailable summaries identify those boundaries; they are not approvals or completed work.
